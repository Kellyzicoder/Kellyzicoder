### 👋 About me

I'm a **Computer Science graduate from the University of Auckland** who likes building things people actually use.

- 🛠️ I build **free tools for real problems**: a photo editor for laptops that can't run Lightroom, a check-in app for my church, and a dashboard that shows how many people are watching our live stream.
- 🎚️ I lead the **Audiovisual (AV) team** that runs sound and screens at events, which is why a lot of my projects are about streaming and live video.
- 🎨 I also **design posters and edit video** as Kelvstudio, so I care a lot about how things look and feel.
- 🔭 Right now I'm looking for **front-end developer roles**.
